## Lab requirements

The following requirements describe the necessary deliverables for the Security Lab.

| Criteria                                                                                                                                           | Points |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Your name, the presentation's title, and the topic are present on an introductory slide.                                                           | 5      |
| The slide deck contains an audio and video presentation of the slide deck's main content.                                                          | 50     |
| The presentation includes an explanation of the topic using terms accessible to a non-technical audience.                                          | 10     |
| The presentation includes details relevant to a non-technical audience about the topic's importance.                                               | 10     |
| The presentation includes details about the actions that those viewing the presentation can take to reduce the risk of harm to their organization. | 10     |
| The slide content supports the verbal presentation instead of being a script.                                                                      | 5      |
| The presentation is delivered within the time limit provided.                                                                                      | 5      |
| Visual elements and design in the presentation are professional.                                                                                   | 5      |
| **Total possible points**                                                                                                                          | 100    |
